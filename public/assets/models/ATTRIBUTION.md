# Aurelia facial rig attribution

`facecap.glb` is the Face Cap 52-morph facial model distributed with the Three.js examples.

- Model credit: Face Cap / Bannaflak
- Distribution source: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf/facecap.glb
- Three.js license: MIT, https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE

The Basis Universal transcoder under `../basis/` is distributed with Three.js and is used locally to decode the model's KTX2 textures.
